This brief describes my personal technical contribution to a product-led digital company. It is not a company marketing document.

1. Product context

Sabo Finance lets users hold and move NGN, GBP, USD, and CAD. Settlement is internal only: users fund Sabo wallets; there are no user-to-user bank payments. Every financial movement must create an append-only ledger row. Wallet balances may be changed only through WalletService inside a TypeORM transaction.

The engineering problem I owned is integrity under attack: forged payment webhooks, stolen JWTs, privilege escalation, unverified trading, and duplicate settlement must fail closed and remain attributable.

2. Architecture (security-critical path)

Client → Bearer JWT → authMiddleware (live user: suspended/deleted)
       → requireVerifiedUser (KYC) → deposits / trades / withdrawals
       → adminMiddleware + requirePermission() → /admin/*

WalletService (FOR UPDATE) → wallets + ledger INSERT (never UPDATE ledger)

Flutterwave → verif-hash (timing-safe) → amount/currency → replay guard
           → security_events → threat score → GET /admin/security/*

Invariant: if any financial step fails, the QueryRunner rolls back. Partial wallet writes are not permitted.
